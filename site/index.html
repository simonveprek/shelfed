A quiet home for your books

Read, organise and convert your books, and send them to your Kindle. Fast, private, and yours. For macOS and Windows.

Download Shelf View the source

Free and open source. No account, no tracking.

Everything a library needs, nothing it doesn't

Your books stay plain files in a folder you can open without the app, one folder per author. Shelf just makes them lovely to live with.

A bookcase you arrange

A real 3D bookcase in paper, oak, walnut or slate. Shelves by author, series or reading status, with a ribbon in every book you're reading.

A reader that remembers

EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, comics and PDF. Highlights, notes, bookmarks and search, and it opens where you stopped.

Your Kindle, recognised

Plug it in and Shelf knows which one it is, from the first Kindle to the Colorsoft, and shows it in its own colour. Drop a book on it to copy it across, or send it by email when it's away.

Plugged in, said hello to

The library steps back, the Kindle rises, the cable goes in and its screen wakes on the book you're reading.

Drag a book onto it

Pick a book off the shelf and drop it on your Kindle. Shelf makes the right format and copies it on.

And the rest

Small things, done carefully.

It starts slowly, on purpose

A book was once the longest conversation you could have with another mind. Shelf opens with that, and then gets out of the way.

Now, read something that takes a while.
